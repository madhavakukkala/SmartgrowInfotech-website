Brand guidelines

The SmartGrow Infotech brand

Everything needed to represent SmartGrow correctly: logos, colors, type, signature elements, and how we write. If you're putting the brand somewhere, start here.

01 · Logo

The circuit star

A five-point star traced like a circuit board: three nodes connected by traces, with the SGI monogram at its heart. It stands for engineering and growth in one mark.

Clear space

Keep space equal to the star's inner node diameter on all sides.

Minimum size

24px tall for the mark, 32px for the horizontal lockup.

Don't recolor

Use only the variants above. No gradients, shadows, or new colors.

Don't distort

Never stretch, rotate, outline, or crop the logo.

02 · Color

04 · Signature elements

The details that make it ours

Circuit eyebrow

Section eyebrows

An orange node, a fading trace, and a mono label. Echoes the circuit star. Opens every major section.

Dotted grid

Blue-tinted dot grid behind heroes, fading out radially. Structure without noise.

Only 2 open slots available!

Availability badge

A pulsing green dot and an honest scarcity line. Always links to the booking page.

05 · Voice

Plain, warm, professional

We write like a person explaining work to another person. Apple-level polish, zero fluff.

Plain English

Short sentences. Everyday words. No jargon unless the reader uses it too.

No em dashes

Commas and full stops carry the rhythm. Never the long dash.

No pricing on pages

Every price conversation happens on a call. Pages never quote fees.

No delivery promises

Timelines depend on the project. They're agreed on the call, not printed.
